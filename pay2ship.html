The application was designed to create an instant shipment quote and invoice generator that allow business owners and merchants to see the cost of moving their cargo,goods or products from one destination to another , using their mobile devices.

Research
To keep my research time as short as possible due to the short timeline i had for this project, i needed to leverage on analysis of 3 shipment company's websites
Competitive Analysis
Impact
Research Insight
• None of the websites shares elaborate information about the types of shipment a user would like to ship.
• 70% has too many information to fill which may increase the bounce rate level on the sites.
• None has complete information about shipment destinations.
Focus of work
User's Needs
• Users should get instant shipment quotes with a few clicks.
• Users should see the cost of their shipment
• Users should have indept understanding about the type of shipment
User Flow
Heuristics Evaluation
Design Heuristics
I tried to speak the users language as part of the heuristics evaluation.
Sometimes users get confused when products communicate to them as bots. I used words, phrases, and concepts familiar to the users, rather than internal jargon. I followed real world conventions, making information appear in a natural and logical order.
I ensured users can understand meanings without having to go look up a word’s definition
Screen Exploration
Visual Exploration
I brainstormed solutions to users needs by filtering, laying out and exploring potential solutions. I try to capture as many aspect of user persona's pain points as i could during this phase. I chose to move forward with the idea to :

• Make users be specific about what they want to ship

• Make users get instant quotes and be able to see the cost of shipments
